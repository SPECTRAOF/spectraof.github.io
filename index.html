About SPECTRA.

SPECTRA is a market analysis platform focused on order flow, volume and market structure. Built to help traders see what is actually happening beneath price.

SPECTRA was born from a simple problem: I couldn't find an order flow platform that worked the way I wanted it to. So I decided to build it myself.

  • Clean, focused interface
  • Deep order flow and volume visualization
  • Highly customizable workspace
  • Built around real trading workflows
  • Less noise, more relevant data

For traders who want to go beyond price action and explore volume, order flow, liquidity and market structure in greater depth.

SPECTRA is currently in development. Built in public, one feature at a time. Follow the build.
