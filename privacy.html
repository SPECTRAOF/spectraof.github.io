Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

1. Data Controller

The data controller for the processing described in this Privacy Policy is Gianluca Di Tardo.

For privacy-related questions or requests, you can contact: privacy@spectraof.com

2. What Data We Collect

When you join the SPECTRA waitlist, we collect the email address you voluntarily provide.

When you visit the website, certain technical information may also be processed by the services used to operate and secure the website, including information such as your IP address, browser information, device information and technical logs.

3. Why We Use Your Data

Your email address is used to:

  • manage your SPECTRA waitlist registration;
  • send a confirmation email after registration;
  • send updates about the development of SPECTRA;
  • communicate information regarding beta access, early access and launch;
  • process unsubscribe and other privacy-related requests.

4. Legal Basis

We process your email address on the basis of your consent when you voluntarily join the SPECTRA waitlist.

You may withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.

5. Email Communications

Emails relating to the SPECTRA waitlist are sent using Resend, an email delivery service operated by Plus Five Five, Inc.

Resend processes email addresses and email-related data on our behalf for the purpose of delivering messages. Resend states that its GDPR processing framework includes a Data Processing Addendum and Standard Contractual Clauses for applicable international transfers.

Resend states that stored customer data is currently located in the United States. The sending region selected for SPECTRA controls where email is routed and sent from, but does not change the location where Resend stores data.

6. Hosting and Infrastructure

The SPECTRA website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub states that visits to GitHub Pages sites may involve the logging and storage of visitor IP addresses for security purposes.

SPECTRA also uses Cloudflare infrastructure, including Cloudflare Workers and Cloudflare D1, to process and store waitlist information.

7. Data Retention

Your email address remains in the SPECTRA waitlist while you remain subscribed to the waitlist, unless we are required to retain it for a longer period by law.

When you unsubscribe, your email address is removed from the SPECTRA waitlist database.

Third-party service providers may retain certain information according to their own applicable retention policies and contractual obligations.

8. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to request:

  • access to your personal data;
  • rectification of inaccurate personal data;
  • erasure of your personal data;
  • restriction of processing;
  • data portability, where applicable;
  • objection to certain processing;
  • withdrawal of consent at any time.

To exercise your rights, contact privacy@spectraof.com.

9. Unsubscribe

Every SPECTRA waitlist email includes an unsubscribe link. You can use that link to remove your email address from the waitlist.

You may also contact privacy@spectraof.com to request deletion of your data.

10. Cookies and Tracking

SPECTRA does not currently use advertising cookies or third-party analytics cookies on the website.

Technical processing and security-related logging may still occur through the infrastructure providers used to operate the website.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the SPECTRA website, services, infrastructure or applicable requirements. The latest version will always be published on this page.

12. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or the processing of your personal data, contact:
