Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

1. Who we are

SPECTRA OF operates SPECTRA, a software platform for market analysis and order-flow tools. For privacy matters, contact privacy@spectraof.com. General enquiries: hello@spectraof.com.

2. Personal data we may process

Depending on the service you use, SPECTRA may process account and contact data such as email address, username and account identifiers; security data such as IP address and login/security events; technical information such as operating system, device information, browser or app version and diagnostic or crash information; communications such as support requests and newsletter preferences; and transaction or billing information supplied by payment providers.

We only intend to collect additional profile or trading-related information where the relevant feature actually requires it and the applicable privacy information has been provided.

3. Why we process data

Processing may be necessary to provide and secure the Service, manage accounts, respond to support requests, maintain and improve reliability, comply with legal obligations, process payments, send requested service communications, and—where required—send newsletters, promotional communications or use optional analytics based on your consent.

4. Legal bases

The legal basis depends on the specific processing activity and may include performance of a contract, compliance with a legal obligation, legitimate interests, or consent. The applicable basis should be assessed for each processing activity in the production environment.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

The website uses necessary technologies and a consent mechanism for optional categories. Optional analytics, preference and marketing technologies must not be activated before the consent required by applicable law is obtained. See the Cookie Policy for details.

6. Service providers

SPECTRA may use infrastructure and service providers such as Cloudflare for website/infrastructure services and Resend for waitlist or email communications. Payment, market-data, analytics and crash-reporting providers will be listed or described when actually deployed. See the Subprocessors page for the production list.

7. International transfers

Some providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where a transfer is made, the applicable transfer mechanism and safeguards will be assessed and documented before the relevant processing is relied upon.

8. Retention

Personal data is retained only for as long as necessary for the relevant purpose, legal obligations, security needs and applicable limitation periods. See the Data Retention Policy.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to certain processing, and to data portability where applicable. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw consent at any time without affecting prior processing. You may also lodge a complaint with the competent data-protection supervisory authority.

10. Security

SPECTRA applies technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risks of the services actually operated. No online service can guarantee absolute security.
