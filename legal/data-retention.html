Data Retention Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Retention periods are tied to purpose, legal obligations, security needs and the technical architecture actually used by SPECTRA. The periods below are operational targets for the current project stage and must be confirmed before production launch.

DataPurposeTarget retention
Account dataService accessUntil account deletion, subject to legal obligations
Deleted-account operational dataSecurity, recovery and deletion workflowsUp to 30 days where technically required
IP and security logsSecurity and abuse preventionUp to 12 months, subject to review
Login/security eventsAccount securityUp to 12 months, subject to review
Crash/diagnostic reportsReliability and debuggingUp to 12 months, subject to provider configuration
AnalyticsMeasurementAccording to the deployed analytics configuration and consent
Support requestsSupport and dispute handlingUp to 24 months, subject to review
Newsletter dataRequested communicationsUntil unsubscribe or removal, subject to legal records
Billing/accounting recordsTax and legal obligationsFor the period required by applicable law
BackupsBusiness continuity and securityLimited backup cycle, targeted at 30–90 days
These are proposed operational targets, not statements that a specific period is legally mandated. Final periods should be reconciled with the production architecture and Italian/EU legal requirements.
